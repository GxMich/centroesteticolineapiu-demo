# Design direction

The promotion joins in-center Esosomic care and an at-home routine. The hero therefore shows a facial-care moment, with the actual Esosomic proposal and direct contact beside it. The official campaign artwork provides the product evidence in the next section.

## Layout

Desktop: a typographic left column and a large portrait occupy the hero. The second section puts the official campaign creative next to the two parts of the offer. Short FAQs precede contact and location information.

Mobile: the offer and both contact actions appear before the large portrait. At 320 px and above, the page has no horizontal overflow. The fixed contact bar appears after the initial controls leave view and hides when the final controls enter view.

## Interaction and evidence

Phone links use the public landline. WhatsApp uses the public Estetica mobile with a prefilled question; nothing is sent automatically. FAQ uses native details/summary. Keyboard focus is visible. The single photo-settle animation is removed for reduced-motion preferences.

The photograph is an editorial illustration. Product packaging and actual campaign details come from the official creative. Price, current promotion validity, and availability are not invented.
